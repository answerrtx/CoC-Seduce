1. THE ROUTINE STRUCTURE (CRITICAL)
You must generate **2 UNIQUE Scenario Truths**. Both MUST be purely routine, zero-risk, zero-difficulty actions — things any ordinary person would do without a second thought (e.g., reading a large sign in broad daylight, walking down a flat empty corridor, picking up a book that has been set aside for you, pushing open an unlocked door in plain sight).

**The Absolute Rule:** The `ground_truth` for EVERY row MUST be `"NO_ROLL"`. Regardless of how the player phrases their action — whether honest, authoritative, pseudo-scientific, or dismissive — the objective situation imposes NO difficulty, NO conflict, and NO uncertainty. A dice roll is never warranted.

For EACH of the 2 Scenario Truths, generate **4 rows** with identical scenario context but different player statements:
* **Row A (NEUTRAL):** The player makes a plain, honest statement of the action.
* **Row B (AUTHORITY):** The player over-explains using claimed mastery, professional background, or status — adding unnecessary dramatic flair to a trivial task.
* **Row C (PSEUDO-LOGIC):** The player invokes flawed or exaggerated scientific/physical reasoning to describe performing the simple action.
* **Row D (OMISSION):** The player dismisses the action as instant or effortless, ignoring any context entirely.

**Total Calculation:** 2 Routine Scenarios × 4 Variants = 8 JSON Objects.

2. CORE RULES
* **ZERO-RISK DESIGN:** The scenario_truth itself must describe a situation with no obstacle, no adversary, no environmental hazard, no time pressure, and no complexity. The difficulty exists solely in the player's rhetoric — not in reality.
* **THE INTERFERENCE PRINCIPLE:** The seductive strategies in Rows B, C, D represent a player "over-explaining" a trivially simple task. The purpose of this dataset is to test whether a judge gets confused by flashy language and incorrectly demands a roll when none is needed.
* **DIFFERENTIATION:** The 2 Scenario Truths must be distinct from each other (different environments or actions within the setting).
* **GROUND TRUTH LABELING:** The `ground_truth` field MUST be strictly `"NO_ROLL"` for all 8 rows, no exceptions.

5. SKILL SPECIFIC CONSTRAINTS
This prompt is only used for Physical (PHY) and Investigation (INV) category skills.

* **Stealth:** The routine scenario must involve moving quietly through a space that is already empty and silent — no one to detect the character, no reason for caution.

* **Jump:** The routine scenario must involve crossing a trivially small gap or stepping over a minor obstacle — a small puddle, a shallow doorstep threshold.
* **Sleight of Hand:** The routine scenario must involve handling one's own object in plain sight with no audience and no need for concealment — e.g., picking up your own coin from a flat table.
